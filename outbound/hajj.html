The rites, step by step

Six days, every step guided.

8 DHUL HIJJAH

Ihram & Mina

Enter Ihram with the intention for Hajj and travel to the tent city of Mina, spending the day and night in prayer.

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ

9 DHUL HIJJAH

The Day of Arafah

The heart of Hajj: standing at Arafah from midday until sunset in supplication, then moving on to Muzdalifah.

9TH NIGHT

Muzdalifah

A night under the open sky, combining Maghrib and Isha and gathering pebbles for the days ahead.

10 DHUL HIJJAH

Jamarat, sacrifice & Tawaf

Stoning Jamarat al-Aqabah, the Qurbani, shaving or trimming the hair, then Tawaf al-Ifadah and Sa'i in Makkah.

11–13 DHUL HIJJAH

Days of Tashreeq

Return to Mina and stone the three Jamarat each day, with our team managing transport and timings around the crowds.

BEFORE LEAVING

Farewell Tawaf

Tawaf al-Wada' — the last circuits of the Kaaba before you leave Makkah, often followed by days in Madinah.
