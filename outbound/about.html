Four chapters

How a small office became two brands.

i.

It started on Dias Place

One desk, one phone line and a promise that every customer would be looked after by a person, not a process. The office in Gunasinghapura, Colombo 12 is still where you'll find us.

ii.

Trusted to do it properly

Over the years we earned IATA accreditation, joined the Travel Agents Association of Sri Lanka and gained approval from the Civil Aviation Authority — the paperwork that lets travellers relax.

iii.

Two brands, one family

Amja Tours shows the world Sri Lanka — tailor-made tours, hotels and our own fleet. Amja Travels takes Sri Lankans to the world — Hajj, Umrah and air tickets everywhere.

iv.

What hasn't changed

We still answer our own phones, we still visit the hotels we recommend, and we still treat a first-time visitor and a twentieth-time pilgrim exactly the same way.
