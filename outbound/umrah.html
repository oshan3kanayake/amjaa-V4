The rites, step by step

Four rites, then Madinah.

AT THE MIQAT

Ihram

Bathe, wear Ihram and make the intention for Umrah before crossing the Miqat — we brief you on when, usually on the flight.

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ عُمْرَةً

MASJID AL-HARAM

Tawaf

Seven circuits of the Kaaba, beginning at the Black Stone, followed by two rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim.

SAFA & MARWAH

Sa'i

Seven walks between the hills of Safa and Marwah, remembering Hajar's search for water.

COMPLETION

Halq or Taqsir

Shaving or trimming the hair completes Umrah — and the rest of your days are for worship in the Haram.

MADINAH

Ziyarah

Prayers at Masjid an-Nabawi, a visit to Quba, Uhud and the historic sites, guided by our team.
